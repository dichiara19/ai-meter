# Trademark policy

The AI Meter **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE): you can use, modify and redistribute it.

The **name "AI Meter" and the AI Meter logo** (the two concentric rings) are trademarks of Giuseppe Gabriele Di Chiara and are not covered by the license (Apache 2.0, section 6). This policy explains how you can use them. Its purpose is that people who download something called AI Meter get the real app: it reads sign-in tokens of other apps, so a modified copy under the same name could hurt users.

## You can, without asking

- Refer to the project by name: in articles, videos, reviews, comparisons, tutorials and package lists, as long as it's clear that you are not the maintainer.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases under the name and logo, for example in a package manager.
- Say that your project is "based on AI Meter" or "a fork of AI Meter".

## You need to change the name and the logo

- If you distribute a **modified version**: a fork, or a build with changes to code, definitions or defaults. Give it a different name and icon, and keep the attribution required by the license (LICENSE and NOTICE).
- If you publish it in an app store under your own account.

## You can't

- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests that the maintainer endorses or sponsors you, your product or your service.
- Use the name or logo for a different product, or in a domain name or account name that could be mistaken for the official project.

For anything not covered here, ask the maintainer, [@dichiara19](https://github.com/dichiara19), through the website linked on the GitHub profile.
