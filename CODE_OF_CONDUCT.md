# Code of Conduct

AI Meter adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, for issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

In short:
- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or sharing of other people's private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer, [@dichiara19](https://github.com/dichiara19), privately through the website linked on their GitHub profile.

Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's privacy is respected. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code of conduct, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
